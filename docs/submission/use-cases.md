# Buyer And Role Use Cases

These are realistic target use cases, not claims of completed outreach or customer adoption.

| Priority | Company / buyer | Daily user | Job FieldLedger supports |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | General contractor / project executive | Project engineer | Reconcile signed change orders, field reports, and cost logs before the weekly owner meeting. |
| 2 | Owner / capital projects director | Owner's representative | Review contractor change requests with exact contract and site evidence. |
| 3 | Electrical subcontractor / operations VP | Project manager | Keep scope, quantity, and allowance changes reviewable from the field without reliable signal. |
| 4 | Construction manager / controls director | Cost engineer | Detect inconsistent approved values and keep one audited change register. |
| 5 | Healthcare system / facilities leader | Program manager | Trace schedule and budget changes across regulated renovation projects. |
| 6 | Public works agency / procurement lead | Contract administrator | Prove which executed document and rule supported each approval. |
| 7 | Renewable developer / construction director | Site manager | Review remote-site evidence offline and stop stale decisions after source replacement. |
| 8 | Insurance carrier / construction claims lead | Claims analyst | Build a cited chronology of quantities, approvals, invoices, and contradictions. |
| 9 | Design-build firm / legal operations | Contract counsel | Inspect unsupported claims and source-version history before dispute correspondence. |
| 10 | Construction software integrator / platform lead | Automation engineer | Drive project, run, review, audit, and export flows through bounded REST or MCP interfaces. |

## Supported Inputs And Domains

The parser supports text-bearing PDF, DOCX paragraphs/tables, UTF-8 TXT, and Markdown. The deterministic rules cover numeric comparisons, equality, and ISO date deadlines. The supplied fixture is construction change control, but the evidence, rule, review, offline queue, and audit pattern can extend to procurement, compliance, insurance, and contract operations after domain-specific evaluation.
